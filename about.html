About us

Eat Your Meat is a whole-animal butcher shop at Kent Ave and N 4th St in Williamsburg, Brooklyn. We opened in 2019.

We buy whole animals from four farms, all within 220 miles of the shop, and cut them up ourselves. We sell nearly every cut, nose to tail, until it’s gone. To see what’s left from this week’s animals, check the board.

Our week

The animals come in on Monday. The shop is closed to walk-ins that day, because that’s when we cut. Dani does the pigs and the chickens, and Marisol the lambs and then the steer, which has already hung 21 days in our cooler. By Monday evening the new board is up, here and online.

We buy the whole animal and pay for all of it, neck and shanks included, so everything on the board stays there until it sells. A steer has one hanger steak. When it’s gone, the next one comes with the next steer.

Butcher’s Boxes are cut on Wednesday. Whatever’s left at the end of the week becomes sausage and stock: the trim goes into the grinder, the bones simmer for 12 hours, and the fat is rendered into tallow.
