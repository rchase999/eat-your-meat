Refunds and replacements

What we refund or replace

If something is wrong with your order, we refund it or replace it. Wrong means:

  • It’s spoiled.
  • It arrived warm.
  • It’s the wrong cut.

How to ask

  1. Contact us within 48 hours of getting your order.
  2. Send a photo of the meat and the packaging.
  3. Include your order number.

Email hello@eatyourmeat.example or call (718) 555-0142. If you think something is spoiled or it arrived warm, don’t cook it.

What we can’t refund

Some things are out of our hands once the meat leaves the shop:

  • A steak cooked past where you wanted it. Our product pages list pull temperatures, and you can call and ask before you cook.
  • Meat left out after delivery, or kept longer than the storage times on the food safety page.
  • A change of mind after delivery. We can’t sell raw meat again once it has left the shop.
  • Weight differences. You pay for actual weight, which is usually within 10% of the estimate. That’s how the price works, not a mistake.
  • An order sent to a wrong address that was entered at checkout. See the shipping policy.

Butcher’s Box

You can skip or cancel before Sunday 11:59 PM Eastern with no fee. After that, that week’s box goes ahead: it’s cut on Wednesday and charged then, at actual weight. If anything in it is wrong, the same rules apply.
