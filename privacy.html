Privacy

Your ticket, orders and gift cards are saved in your own browser, on your device. Clearing your browser’s data for this site deletes them.

What we’d collect and why

  • Name and delivery address, to get the order to you.
  • Phone number, to text you when the courier leaves and to call if there’s a problem with your order.
  • Email, to contact you about your order or your Butcher’s Box.

Card details go to our payment processor. We don’t store them.

Who we’d share it with

  • The payment processor, to charge your card.
  • Our courier, for same-evening orders: the name, address and phone number on the order.
  • The shipping carrier, for overnight orders: the name, address and phone number on the order.

Each gets what it needs for its part of the job and nothing more. We don’t sell your data.

Cookies and storage

The site stores only what your ticket needs, in your browser. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Deleting your information

Email hello@eatyourmeat.example or call (718) 555-0142 and ask. We’ll tell you what we have and delete it.
